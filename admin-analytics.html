Why Deep Learning ANN was selected over alternative models:
  • Random Forest Overfitting: While Random Forest achieved 1.513 Train RMSE, its Test RMSE surged to 3.680 (+143% error inflation), indicating high variance and memorization of training profiles[cite: 18].
  • Linear Ridge Limitation: Ridge regression was bounded at 2.183 Test RMSE because linear models cannot model the compound, non-linear interaction between external transit/work hours and classroom test outputs[cite: 18].
  • ANN Generalization: The ANN maintained tight parity between Train RMSE (1.875) and Test RMSE (1.916), demonstrating optimal bias-variance tradeoff and providing the highest reliability for institutional academic interventions[cite: 18].
  • Qualitative Integration: Beyond raw percentages, the model adjusts projected academic trajectory using voluntary qualitative factors (emotional wellbeing, travel fatigue, employment shifts), allowing faculty advisers to interpret context rather than relying solely on grade cutoffs[cite: 18].
